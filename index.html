Selected Research

01 Source-Based Morphometry-Derived Individualized Structural Covariance Networks Reveal Neuroanatomical Heterogeneity in Major Depressive Disorder
Changmin Chen et al.
Under review at Journal of Affective Disorders

A large-scale multisite MRI study identifying three neuroanatomical subtypes of major depressive disorder, primarily distinguished by altered connections between the frontoparietal control and default mode networks.

02 Beyond Stationarity: A Hilbert–Huang Transform Framework for Reliable, Frequency-Resolved Analysis of Resting-State Brain Networks
Yuhan Liu, Wenyao Li, Changmin Chen, and Qing Zhao
Under review at NeuroImage · OHBM 2026

An adaptive time-frequency framework revealing reproducible frequency and energy profiles across resting-state brain networks.

03 Abnormal Structural Covariance Network in Major Depressive Disorder: Evidence from the REST-meta-MDD Project
Changmin Chen, Yuhan Liu, Yu Sun, Wenhao Jiang, Yonggui Yuan, Qing Zhao, and the DIRECT Consortium
NeuroImage: Clinical, 2025
Paper · Abstract and Poster

A large-scale multisite MRI study identifying frontal-centered disruption of structural covariance networks in major depressive disorder.

04 Causal Structural Covariance Network Revealing Progressive Changes in Major Depressive Disorder
Changmin Chen and Qing Zhao
OHBM 2025
Abstract and Poster

A disease-progression-based analysis revealing directed structural alterations across frontal, temporal, and medial prefrontal regions, with distinct patterns in first-episode and recurrent MDD.

05 UniSPAC: A Unified Segmentation Framework for Proofreading and Annotation in Connectomics
J. Deng, Q. Wu, Changmin Chen, Q. Zheng, Z. Zhang, J. Wu, W. Ouyang, and C. Song
bioRxiv, 2024 · Under review at Nature Methods
Preprint

A unified 2D–3D framework for efficient connectomics segmentation, proofreading, and neuron tracing.
